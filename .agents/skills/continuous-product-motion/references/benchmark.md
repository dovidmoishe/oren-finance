## What the film demonstrates

The message is: ask about a stock, see perspectives from different sources, and understand both sides. The original feature review informed the film; the graphic uses conceptual themes, not fabricated posts, quotations, counts, or sentiment scores.

- **0–1.8 s:** NVDA badge and connected X/newsletter/podcast icons establish the subject before chat. The short line is “Beyond the chart.”
- **1.8–5.5 s:** camera brings in a large composer. One question types: “What are people saying about NVDA?” The pointer presses Send, the composer gives way to the sent state, and reading dots appear briefly.
- **5.5–9 s:** “Follow the sources.” Three source cards use crisp icons and short labels. One receives a restrained lift.
- **9–12.5 s:** “Both sides.” Six small opinion-theme tiles fan into opposing groups around the NVDA hub. Curved connectors draw in; a small marker resolves beneath them. One tile on each side receives a subtle emphasis.
- **12.5–17 s:** move into the Oren mascot and “A clearer view.” The official Powered by Drip lockup appears only at the close.

These are an example rhythm, not a mandatory five-scene template. A different feature needs its own action and result.

## Choices that improved the film

**Less writing.** Early versions overexplained research and added a writing/saving-a-thesis beat. Removing them made the film easier to follow. Do not recreate rejected content merely because it exists in an early source file.

**A visual opening.** Starting immediately inside chat felt abrupt and generic. The stock-and-source relationship gave the first shot meaning and created an object to follow into the interface.

**Pacing through subtraction.** The 22-second version had long holds. The 17-second version shortened settled sections while preserving the fast-arrival, soft-settle movement.

**A composed score.** A newly generated sparse electronic cue still felt boring. A second original cue used an immediate bass/drum groove and repeating synth hook. “New audio” alone did not solve the problem; musical energy and structure did.

**Substance in the comparison.** Two large Bullish/Bearish icon cards felt basic. The approved revision kept the labels minimal but made the evidence relationship visible: AI demand, Growth, and Moat on one side; Valuation, Competition, and Execution on the other. The source icons tie this scene to the preceding one. Do not copy finance themes into unrelated products.

**Stable identity.** Stock, source symbols, borders, typography, and restrained accent colors persist across scenes. Richer detail did not require a new visual language.

## Concrete craft values

- Shared world: approximately 5200×5100; scene zones 1920×1080.
- Camera framing: world origin at 0,0, translated so a world-space target lands at frame center.
- Principal scene travel: about 1.05 seconds, expo.out, arriving at 1.025–1.04 scale; a 0.55–0.60 second resolving move returns to scale 1.
- Typical reveal: 18 px rise, 0.96→1 scale, 7→0 px blur, power4.out, approximately 0.55 seconds, 60 ms stagger.
- Comparison fan: opposing horizontal offsets of ±150 px, 15 px rise, 0.70 seconds, 70 ms stagger. These are larger narrative movements than a normal UI entrance.
- Connector draw: 0.68 seconds, approximately 45 ms stagger. Balance marker moves once and resolves; it does not run an endless loop or represent a measured numerical score.
- Press: 1→0.96 in 0.15 seconds, release to 1 in 0.25 seconds.

Use values as starting points. The final appearance depends on framing, scale, overlap, contrast, and reading time, not a particular easing string.

## Verified final delivery

17.000 seconds, 1920×1080 H.264, 60 fps, 1,020 frames; AAC audio also 17 seconds. Measured integrated audio: −16.08 LUFS; true peak: −4.18 dBTP. These describe this file, not required loudness settings for every film.

Ten encoded frame samples: 0.8, 1.95, 4.85, 7.5, 9.3, 9.8, 10.4, 11.2, 12.8, and 15.2 seconds. More dense sampling or playback is needed to assess motion continuity and music synchronization. The benchmark's clean settled stills are useful visual evidence, not proof that every possible handoff is flawless.
